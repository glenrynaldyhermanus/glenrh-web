Lobby Land

A small multiplayer game: pick a character, fight the AI solo, or open a room and share the code with a friend.

45

How to play

Play Lobby Land.

Press Play, then choose a mode. Solo puts you against the AI. Create room gives you a code to share, and a friend on another device can join with it.

There’s a tower defense mode too, for when nobody else is around.

Lobby Land Full screen

About 15 MB. It loads when you press Play.

This page needs JavaScript to load the game. You can also open Lobby Land on its own.

Behind it

Lobby Land is Flutter and Flame on the client, with Supabase Realtime keeping every player in the same room in sync.

It’s a playground for game networking: lobbies, room codes and rematches, built small enough to finish.
