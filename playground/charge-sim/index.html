Charge Sim

Pick a charger, scan its QR, pay with pretend QRIS and watch the kilowatt-hours roll in. Time runs a lot faster here.

39

How to play

Charge a car by QR.

Follow the screens: choose a charger, scan, pick an amount and pay. Then plug in, or wait and see what happens when you don’t.

Charging stops by itself at the amount you paid for, or when the battery is full. Anything left over goes back to the wallet.

Behind it

This is the exact flow from ShtEV, with the same three safety nets: cut off at the quota, cancel after 90 seconds without a cable, refund the rest. The real one talks to chargers over Tuya Cloud and OCPP.
