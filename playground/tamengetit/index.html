Tamengetit

A pocket pet in the spirit of the Tamagotchi. Hatch the egg, feed it, keep it clean and keep it alive.

43

How to play

Play Tamengetit.

Press Play, then click inside the game. Feed your pet at meal times, give it something spicy for fun, clean up after it and bring medicine when it’s sick.

The game is in Indonesian, just like the original. Your pet is saved in this browser, so it’ll be waiting next time.

Tamengetit Full screen

About 15 MB. It loads when you press Play.

This page needs JavaScript to load the game. You can also open Tamengetit on its own.

Behind it

Tamengetit is a real Flutter app built with the Flame game engine, compiled for the web. The same code runs on Android and iOS.

The pet is drawn from SVG parts and recoloured, so every egg hatches a slightly different monster. Read the short story
