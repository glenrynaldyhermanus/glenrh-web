Warung Order

Order from Glen’s pretend warung the way you’d say it out loud, in Indonesian. It turns your sentence into a receipt.

37

How to play

Order in plain Indonesian.

Type an order the way you’d say it, like dua nasi goreng pedas sama satu es teh. Number words and nicknames such as nasgor work.

On browsers that support it, press the microphone and say it instead. Speech is recognised in Indonesian (id-ID).
