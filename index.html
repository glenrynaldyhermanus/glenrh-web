Once upon a time, in Indonesia,

There was a developer called Glen Rynaldy Hermanus. He builds apps for phones, desktops and the web, and he usually builds the whole thing: the server, the screens and the last little button.

What he makes

Apps people actually open.

  • Native mobileKotlin with Jetpack Compose, Swift with SwiftUI
  • Cross-platformFlutter, from a baby kick tracker to a padel club POS
  • Backends & agentsGo services, PostgreSQL, WebSockets and AI agents
  • Web dashboardsNext.js, SvelteKit and Prisma
  • DesktopSwiftUI on macOS, WinUI 3 on Windows
  • Automationn8n workflows that do the boring parts
How he works

Three habits you’ll see in every chapter.

  1. Start with one real problem.

    Not a platform, not a roadmap. One person, one annoying problem, today.

  2. Ship something you can hold.

    An APK on a phone, a link that opens, a build on a desk. Feedback beats opinions.

  3. Sweat the last ten percent.

    Empty states, motion, the tiny copy under a button. That’s where trust lives.
