m.tix

The mobile app for Indonesia’s largest cinema chain: tickets, snacks and private screenings, in one pocket.

13

The setting

A night at the movies starts on a phone.

Cinema XXI runs the biggest cinema network in Indonesia. For most moviegoers the night starts in m.tix: pick a cinema, pick a film, pick a seat, add popcorn.

When I joined, the job was the app. Over time it grew into everything behind it: the core systems, the POS at the outlet counter and the tools our own team uses every day.

The quest

More than a ticket.

The home screen puts the four things people come for up front: Cinema, Movies, m.food and Private Booking. Now playing sits right under them.

After you pay, the order turns into a moment. Special screenings come with a collectible ticket, and some orders hide a gift you scratch to reveal.

The craft

One app, a whole cinema behind it.

The app is Flutter on Android and iOS. Behind it is a Go API for the core, a web POS that the outlet counters use, and Cineflow, a desktop copilot I built for our product team on Mac and Windows.

Cineflow puts the ticket, an AI coding helper and visual QA on one screen. A moved button is caught by a screenshot diff before the audience ever sees it.

The ending

m.tix is live across Indonesia on Android and iOS. The design is the part I’m proudest of: dark, calm and a little bit cinematic.

Leading it taught me that the app is only the front door. The business lives in the core, at the counter and in the tools the team uses every day.
