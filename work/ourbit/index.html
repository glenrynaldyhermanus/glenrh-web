Ourbit

POS and inventory for retail: the cashier, the stockroom and an online store, sold as one starter kit.

29

The setting

Small shops want to go digital, fast.

A small shop needs a cashier, a stock count, a way to buy from suppliers and, these days, an online store. Buying four tools for that is expensive and they never talk to each other.

Ourbit sells it as one starter kit: everything a new business needs from day one.

Role
Product and engineering
When
2025
Platforms
POS, web, backend
The quest

The counter, the stockroom and the storefront.

  1. A cashier app.

    Ring up sales on a tablet or a PC, and every sale moves the stock on its own.

  2. Inventory and purchasing.

    Products, stock, suppliers and purchase orders, with a simple finance view on top.

  3. An online store.

    A ready-made shop on the web that sells from the same stock as the counter.

The craft

One database, many counters.

A Go API with chi and pgx sits on a PostgreSQL schema that covers POS, inventory, purchasing, finance and the online store. The cashier is Flutter, and the CMS and storefront are Next.js.

The same family also includes Ourank, for HR.

  • Go
  • chi
  • PostgreSQL
  • Flutter
  • Next.js
  • Prisma
  • Firebase Hosting
The ending

The cashier and the CMS sit behind a login, so this story is told with a map instead of screens.

Building it taught me that “POS” is never just a cashier. It’s stock, money and suppliers, and they have to agree.
