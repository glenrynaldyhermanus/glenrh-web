Metropole Padel

An integrated platform for a padel club and its restaurant: book a court, reserve a table, check in at the counter.

15

The setting

A club is two businesses.

Metropole Padel Club has championship courts and Candlenut, a restaurant right next to them. Players book a court, then stay for dinner.

That means two kinds of booking, two kinds of cashier and one owner who wants to see all of it in one place.

The quest

From the court to the kitchen.

Players pick a date and a start time, then a court, by time or by court, with add-ons after. The same account reserves a table or a private room at the restaurant.

At the club, a court POS checks players in and out, and the restaurant POS stays in sync with the kitchen’s own system. The owner sees courts and food on one dashboard.

The craft

Many counters, one club.

Go services own courts, bookings and food. Players use the web or the native apps. Staff use a Flutter POS on a PC or tablet, and a small Windows app syncs the restaurant’s menu and sales with its local POS.

The ending

The club runs on it today. Try a booking yourself at metropolepadelclub.com.

The lesson: a club is not one app. It’s a court, a kitchen and an owner, and they all need the same truth.
