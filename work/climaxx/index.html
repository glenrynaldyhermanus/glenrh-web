Climaxx

A short-drama production studio, plus the app people watch it on. Write the bible, cast the characters, then shoot a season.

23

The setting

Short dramas need a lot of episodes.

Vertical short dramas live on phones, in quick episodes, and one series needs dozens of them. That is a lot of production for a small team.

Climaxx started as Filpen in 2025, a streaming app where viewers choose what happens next. It became a studio that makes the series and an app that shows them.

Role
Solo: product to pipeline
When
From 2025
Platforms
macOS, Android, iOS, web
Status
In active development
The quest

Cast first, shoot later.

A writer describes a movie and the studio drafts the story bible: seasons, episodes and scenes. Each scene gets a still to approve, and only approved stills go into the video queue.

Every character has a locked face and a costume sheet drawn from four views, so the same person looks the same in episode one and episode twenty-five.

The craft

A Go core with many cameras.

The Go core owns the bible, the cast, the stills and the generation jobs, and it recovers running jobs after a restart. The studio is native on the Mac, and the viewer apps are native on each phone.

  • Go
  • PostgreSQL
  • SwiftUI
  • Kotlin
  • SvelteKit
  • Next.js
  • Generative AI
The ending

Climaxx is in active development. The character sheet above came out of it, unretouched.

Consistency turned out to be a data problem more than a model problem: write it down once, then make every step read it.
