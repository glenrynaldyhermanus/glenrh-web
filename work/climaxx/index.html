Climaxx

An AI production studio for short series: write the bible, cast the characters, then generate a season.

19

The setting

Consistent characters are hard.

Generating one pretty image is easy. Keeping the same face and the same costume across a whole season of episodes is not.

Climaxx treats a series like a real production: a story bible first, a cast with fixed identities, then the episodes.

Role
Solo: product to pipeline
When
October 2026
Platforms
macOS, Android, iOS, web
Status
In active development
The quest

Cast first, shoot later.

Every character gets a locked face and a costume sheet drawn from four views: T-pose, side, back and overhead. Later shots must agree with those.

Writers describe a movie, add seasons and press generate. The studio writes the season from the story bible, shows live progress, and can be recalled or regenerated.

The craft

A Go core with many cameras.

The Go core owns the bible, the cast, the stills and the generation jobs, and it recovers running jobs after a restart. Each client is native to its platform.

  • Go
  • PostgreSQL
  • SwiftUI
  • Kotlin
  • SvelteKit
  • Next.js
  • Generative AI
The ending

Climaxx is in active development. The character sheet above came out of it, unretouched.

Consistency turned out to be a data problem more than a model problem: write it down once, then make every step read it.
