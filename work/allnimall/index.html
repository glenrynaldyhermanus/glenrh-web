Allnimall

A one-stop platform for the pet industry: the shop counter, the vet, the collar and the community.

19

The setting

Pet care was all over the place.

Pet owners juggle a pet shop, a vet, a groomer and a chat group. The shops and clinics on the other side often run on notebooks and spreadsheets.

Allnimall started in 2022 with one idea: put the whole pet industry on one platform, from the owner’s phone to the shop’s counter.

Role
Product and engineering, end to end
When
From 2022
Platforms
Android, iOS, web, POS
Status
Live
Site
pet.allnimall.com
The quest

Four doors into one business.

  1. A POS for vets and pet shops.

    Sales, services, stock, staff and payments, on a desktop, a tablet or the web.

  2. On-demand services.

    Owners book care like grooming and vet visits from their phone.

  3. A smart QR collar.

    Each collar links to the pet’s profile and health records. If someone finds a lost pet and scans it, the owner gets the location right away.

  4. Communities.

    A social feed and adoption for pet lovers, and Peduli Kucing for people who look after street cats.

The craft

One backend, many small apps.

Every app is small and focused, and they all share one Supabase backend. The POS is Flutter, so the same build runs at the counter, on a tablet and in a browser. The store CMS is Next.js, with Midtrans for payments.

  • Flutter
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Next.js
  • Firebase
  • Midtrans
  • QR codes
The ending

The collar is live at pet.allnimall.com. The POS, the store CMS and the community apps are built around it.

Allnimall taught me to build a business in pieces. Each app does one job, and the backend keeps them telling the same story.
