Transmore

Field acquisition for retail networks: send people to stores, get photos and data back, pay them for it.

27

The setting

The field is where retail happens.

Brands want their products, posters and displays in thousands of small stores. Somebody has to visit each one, set things up and prove it was done.

That proof used to arrive as paper forms and photos in a chat group. Transmore turns it into a task, a check-in and a report.

Role
Mobile app and CMS
When
2024
Platforms
Android, iOS, web
Status
Client project
The quest

From a task to a paid visit.

  1. The office plans.

    A CMS holds clients, projects, stores, goods and field workers, with every store on a map.

  2. The field checks in.

    Workers get tasks in the app, scan the store’s QR, take photos and fill the form, even when a visit can’t be finished.

  3. Everyone sees the result.

    Results sync in real time, and each finished task lands in the worker’s wallet.

The craft

Fast to build, easy to change.

Both the app and the CMS were built in FlutterFlow on Firebase, so the client could see a working build early and change forms without a rewrite. Cloud Functions handle the parts that must not run on a phone.

  • FlutterFlow
  • Flutter
  • Firebase
  • Firestore
  • Cloud Functions
  • QR codes
The ending

Transmore lives behind a login for field teams, so there are no public screens to share here.

It was MoGawe’s old idea, done again with better tools: the field is a business, and every visit is a row of data.
