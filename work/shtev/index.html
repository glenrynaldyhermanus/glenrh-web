ShtEV

A commercial EV charging ecosystem: a driver app, an owner portal and a backend that turns wall chargers into a business.

21

The setting

Chargers with no one standing next to them.

ShtEV puts wall chargers in apartments, offices and parking lots. Nobody stands next to them, so the charger itself has to sell the electricity, safely.

The people who own the chargers need it to work like a business: set the tariff, watch the network, see the money come in.

Role
Client project, full stack
When
2026
Platforms
Android, iOS, web, backend
Status
Live
Client
shtevapp.com
The quest

One side for drivers, one side for owners.

Drivers scan the charger’s QR, choose an amount, pay with QRIS and watch charging live. The power cuts off exactly at the paid quota, the session cancels itself if the cable isn’t plugged in within 90 seconds, and any unused balance goes back to the wallet.

Owners get a portal: a live map of every charger, its status, tariffs and margins, transactions and invoices.

The craft

Mock the hardware, then swap it.

Payments and chargers sit behind interfaces. The whole flow ran on mocks first, then the real Tuya Cloud and OCPP adapters slotted in without touching the screens.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • SwiftUI
  • Go
  • SvelteKit
  • Tailwind CSS
  • QRIS
  • OCPP
  • Tuya
The ending

The backend and the owner portal are live, and the public site lives at shtevapp.com.

You can try the whole flow, timeout and refund included, in the Charge Sim mini app.
